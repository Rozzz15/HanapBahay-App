# Privacy Policy Draft for HanapBahay

*This is a draft. You should review this and host it on a simple website (like GitHub Pages or a Google Doc) before submitting to the Play Store.*

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **HanapBahay**. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, or otherwise when you contact us.

- **Account Data:** Name, email address, phone number, and password.
- **Property Data:** Images, descriptions, and location of properties you list.
- **Payment Data:** We use PayMongo for payments. We do not store your full card details on our servers; they are handled securely by PayMongo.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use personal information collected via our App for a variety of business purposes, including:
- To facilitate account creation and logon process.
- To enable property listings and user-to-user communication.
- To process payments for bookings.
- To manage user accounts.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
Your data is stored securely using:
- **Railway:** For our primary database (PostgreSQL).
- **Supabase:** For media storage (images and videos).
All data is stored in the cloud to ensure persistence and security.

## 5. Sharing Your Information
We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations.

## 6. Your Rights
You may review, change, or terminate your account at any time by contacting us.
